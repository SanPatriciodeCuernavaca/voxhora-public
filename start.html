Welcome 👋

Three steps, in this order. About five minutes, nothing to configure.

Keep your code handy — you'll enter it again on your iPhone (step 2) and in the app's signup.

1

On your Mac — download Voxhora

Click below, then on the page that opens click the .dmg file. Open it and drag Voxhora-Mac into Applications. The first launch opens the signup wizard — it will ask for this same code.

Download for Mac

If your Mac hesitates to open it the first time: right-click the app → Open. It's notarized by Apple and safe.

2

On your iPhone & iPad — get the app

Open voxhora.app/start on your iPhone, enter the same code, and tap below — it installs through Apple's TestFlight. Your Apple Watch installs itself from your iPhone.

Apple emails your personal TestFlight invitation separately — tap Accept, then Install. Your Apple Watch installs itself from your iPhone.

Get it on iPhone (TestFlight)
3

Connect your Voxhora AI

Once the app is installed on any one device, tap below on that device (or let the signup wizard do it with your code). One tap, one device — everything else syncs on its own.
