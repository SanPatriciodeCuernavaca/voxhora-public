Welcome 👋

Four steps, in this order. Take your time. If anything at all is unclear, stop and call or email me and I'll do it with you.

Keep your code handy — you will type it once more, in the setup assistant on your Mac (step 1).

1

Your Mac

Click the button. Your browser opens a page with one file ending in .dmg — click it to download, then open it from your Downloads. A window appears with the Voxhora icon; drag that icon onto the Applications folder beside it. Then open Voxhora from your Applications folder.

The first time you open Voxhora, a setup assistant starts on its own and walks you through, one screen at a time:

  1. A welcome screen — click through it.
  2. It checks that iCloud is turned on, so your work reaches your other devices.
  3. It asks your Mac’s permission for notifications and to read your court email.
  4. It asks for your access code. Type the code above and click Connect. That is the only thing you type, and only once on this Mac.
  5. Then your TechShare login for discovery, a check of your court email, and where to keep your files.

Any screen you would rather handle later has a Skip for now button, and your progress is saved if you quit partway.

Download Voxhora for Mac

If your Mac says it can't check the app, right-click the Voxhora icon and choose Open — then click Open again. It's signed and approved by Apple; this only happens once.

2

Your iPhone and iPad

Voxhora installs on your iPhone and iPad through Apple’s TestFlight app (it installs TestFlight for you if you do not have it). Do this on each device.

Apple sends you a TestFlight invitation by email — open it on your iPhone, tap Accept, then Install. Do the same on your iPad.

Point your iPhone camera here

Easiest way: open your iPhone camera and point it at this square. Tap the link that pops up, and TestFlight takes it from there.

Or open the install link here

In TestFlight, turn on Automatic Updates — then future versions arrive by themselves. Your iPhone does not ask for the code; it picks everything up from your Mac.

3

Your Apple Watch

On your iPhone, open the app called Watch (it comes with every iPhone — the black icon with a watch on it). Scroll down to Available Apps, find Voxhora, and tap Install. If Voxhora is already under "Installed on Apple Watch," you're done — nothing to do.

The Watch has no code to enter — it picks everything up from your iPhone.

4

You're done — here's what happens by itself

You entered your code once. Your Voxhora AI, your clients, your calendar, and your billing now travel between your Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Watch on their own — you don't set anything up twice.

One thing to check on each device: Settings → your name → iCloud, and make sure iCloud Keychain and iCloud Drive are turned on. That's what carries everything across.

If your AI ever asks to be connected on a device, just type the same code again — it never runs out of devices.
