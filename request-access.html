Request access

Voxhora is invite-only during the beta. Tell us who you are and, once approved, Patrick sends you a short access code. Take it to voxhora.app/start — that one page walks you through everything: Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and your Voxhora AI. Approvals are manual, typically same-day.

Your request goes straight to Patrick — nothing opens on your computer, and you don't need email set up on this device. Prefer to write instead? Email patrick@patrickfagerberg.com with your name and the Apple ID email you use on your iPhone.

What happens next

  1. We review your request — usually same-day.
  2. You get one welcome email with everything: the Mac download, your TestFlight invite for iPhone/iPad/Apple Watch, and a one-tap link that turns on Voxhora AI (you never type a key).
  3. Once your code arrives, go to voxhora.app/start — enter it there and your install steps appear, in order, for every device.
  4. Sign in on each device with the same Apple ID — your clients, calendar, and billing sync everywhere automatically.

Why TestFlight for the mobile apps

The iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch apps are delivered through TestFlight, Apple's official app-distribution program — free, secure, and bundled with iCloud. You install Apple's TestFlight app once, accept your invite, and Voxhora installs and auto-updates from there. (The Mac app installs directly from the download link in your welcome email.)
